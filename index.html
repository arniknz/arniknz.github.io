📺 How to use this TV (click to fold)
  1. Pick a channel. CH-1 plots y=f(x), CH-2 parametric x(t),y(t), CH-3 polar r(θ). Switching channels loads a working demo — your old formula uses a different variable, so it can't follow.
  2. Type a formula and watch it grow pixel by pixel. Gaps (like tan(x) at π/2) stay open — no fake vertical lines.
  3. Make sliders appear: use any letter besides x, e.g. a·sin(b·x) gives you knobs for a and b.
  4. Steer the picture: mouse wheel = zoom, drag = pan, two fingers = pinch on phones. The right TV knob zooms too (drag it up/down, double-click resets).
  5. Copy link builds a URL that contains your formulas, sliders and view after the # — send it to a friend and they see exactly your screen.
